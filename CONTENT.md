## How it works (/how-it-works)

- H1: Four steps. No guesswork.
- Subhead: Every BIAB operator runs the same loop, whether Solo or Duo.

**01 — Apply & onboard**
Tell us whether you're starting Solo or Duo. You get access to the playbook, the scoring criteria, and your first monthly batch within days.

**02 — Receive 250 scored leads**
Every month, 250 real Swiss trade businesses — Sanitär, Elektrik, Malerei, Garten, and more — each scored on outdated design, missing mobile support, and site age.

**03 — Pitch & close**
Work the list with the outreach playbook: what to say, what to show, and how to price a first project versus a retainer.

**04 — Deliver & scale**
Build the site, deliver the result, and turn the client into a monthly retainer. Repeat with the next batch — or bring on a second Primary Owner.

**What "scored" actually means**
Every lead comes from the Swiss federal commercial register, then gets ranked against a checklist built for outreach, not vanity: how outdated the business's current site is, whether it works on mobile, how complete its public information is, and how good a fit its legal form and size are for a first-time web client. You work down a ranked list — not a raw export.

## FAQ (/faq)

- H1: Frequently asked questions

1. **What exactly is a "lead" here?**
   A real, named Swiss company — legal name, address, canton, and business purpose pulled from the federal commercial register — scored on how outdated or incomplete its current web presence is. It is not a cold list of random emails; every lead is a specific business with a specific, visible reason to need what you're selling.

2. **Do I need design or development skills to start?**
   You need to be able to deliver a website, whether that's you personally, a no-code builder, or a subcontractor. BIAB supplies the leads, the pitch, and the scoring — not the build itself. Many Duo pairs split exactly along this line: one Primary Owner sells, the other builds.

3. **How is Duo different from just splitting a Solo plan with a friend?**
   Duo gives both people a Primary Owner seat with full access to the list and playbook, priced per person. Splitting a single Solo seat means one of you doesn't actually have direct access to leads or support — which tends to break down exactly when you need clear ownership of who's talking to which client.

4. **Is this market already saturated?**
   No single agency can serve more than a small slice of the Swiss trade and service sector — it's tens of thousands of businesses across every canton. The constraint has never been demand for better websites; it's been finding the specific businesses that need one and reaching them before someone else does.

5. **What if I don't close anything in month one?**
   That's within the normal range — see the realistic timeline on our how-it-works page. Month one is largely outreach and pitch refinement. If you're still at zero by the end of month two, talk to us; that's usually a pitch or targeting issue we can help you fix.

6. **Can I cancel anytime?**
   Yes. There's no lock-in contract on either plan. If you cancel, you keep everything you've already built with existing clients — BIAB only supplies the ongoing monthly lead batch.

7. **Can I switch from Solo to Duo later?**
   Yes, this is the most common upgrade path. Most operators start Solo, hit a delivery-capacity ceiling somewhere between month six and nine, and bring on a second Primary Owner from there.
